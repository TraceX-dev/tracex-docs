## Accessing your workspace

You can access your TraceX workspace through a browser on your mobile device. Some views may not be optimized for smaller screens, but you can access chat, inbox and other modules.

## Receiving notifications

On iPhone, use Safari to add the TraceX web app to your Home Screen, then enable web push notifications. You can manage notification preferences in your workspace's [notification settings](/communication/notifications/).
