TraceX brings quality management, product development and team collaboration into one platform. It helps teams manage quality processes, product documentation, development and test activities throughout the product lifecycle.

Designed for teams in regulated industries, TraceX includes controlled documents, review and approval workflows, electronic signatures, PDF export, trainings and product management. Task tracking, virtual office, chat, wiki documents and file storage help your team collaborate alongside these quality processes.

This guide covers the modules and features available in TraceX. See the [workspace setup guide](/getting-started/workspace-setup) to configure your workspace, or visit <a href="https://tracex.co/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">tracex.co</a> for more information.
